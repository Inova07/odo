Getting started

Odo tracks your delivery and rideshare shifts, the kilometres you drive for work and your work expenses. It then works out your tax deductions and gives you an accountant-ready summary at tax time.

Install it on your phone so it opens like a normal app, full screen and offline:

  1. iPhone: open the Odo link in Safari → tap the Share button (square with an arrow) → Add to Home Screen → Add.
  2. Android: open the link in Chrome → tap the ⋮ menu → Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Open Odo from the new icon on your home screen from now on.

First-time setup (1 minute):

  1. Tap the ⚙ Settings button (top right).
  2. Check Region is Australia. This sets the ATO km rate, 5,000 km cap and the July–June financial year.
  3. Enter your expected total income for the year (deliveries plus any other job — a rough guess is fine). Odo picks your tax bracket for you.
  4. Check Your vehicles: choose Car, Motorbike or Bicycle. Bike riders get Rider mode, focused on the costs you can claim.
  5. Tap Save settings.
  6. Optional but recommended: tap the 👤 Account button and create a free account so your records are backed up.
💡 You can replay the quick tour at any time from Settings → How to use Odo.

Logging a shift

A shift is the main thing you record in Odo. Each one stores the date, platform, kilometres, earnings and hours.

Live shift (best way):

  1. When you start working, tap Start shift on the Home screen.
  2. Choose the platform (Uber, DoorDash, Menulog…) and type your car's starting odometer reading. Pro users can tap Start reading to snap a photo of the odometer instead.
  3. Tap Clock on. A green dot and a running timer show you're on shift. You can close the app, because the timer keeps going.
  4. When you finish, tap End shift. Enter your ending odometer reading and what you earned. Hours are filled in for you.
  5. Tap Save shift. Your estimated tax back updates straight away.
💡 Free plan: one live shift (Start → End) per day. Doing a second shift? Snap your odometer, then add it manually afterwards — manual entries are always unlimited. Odo Pro removes the limit.

Forgot to start a shift? Go to the Shifts tab → Add shift and enter it afterwards. If you don't have odometer readings, switch to Enter km and type the distance from your platform's trip history.

Working several apps at once? Log one shift, pick the main platform, enter your combined earnings and use the Note field to list the others.

💡 Take a photo of your odometer at the start and end of each shift. Your camera roll then backs up your records if the ATO ever asks.
⚠️ Only kilometres driven for work count. The trip from home to your first job, and home again afterwards, may be private travel. If you're not sure, check with the ATO or a registered tax agent.

Editing: tap any shift (or expense) to change it — fix a typo in earnings, add a photo, change the platform. There's a Delete link at the bottom too.

Paid weekly? Add a week. On the Shifts tab tap + Week and copy the totals from your weekly Uber or DoorDash statement. A handy routine: log shifts live for the kilometres (leave earnings blank), then add the week's earnings from the statement. Leave distance blank on the week entry so kilometres aren't counted twice.

Forgot to end a shift? If a live shift runs for 12+ hours, Odo asks whether you forgot. You can end it and type the hours you really worked, keep going, or discard it.

Deleting: tap the bin icon on any shift, then tap the tick to confirm. An Undo button appears for a few seconds.

Logging expenses

Use the Expenses tab for work costs other than driving: your phone bill, delivery bags, parking, tolls and so on.

  1. Tap Add expense.
  2. Pick a category. Odo suggests a typical business-use percentage.
  3. Enter the amount and date.
  4. Adjust the business use slider. If you use your phone 70% for work and 30% personally, set it to 70%. Odo only counts the work share.
  5. Add a note (store name, what it was) and Save.
⚠️ Don't double-dip. The ATO cents-per-km rate already covers fuel, rego, insurance, servicing, repairs and depreciation. If you claim your kilometres, don't also claim those car costs, or Odo will show a warning on the Export screen. Parking and tolls can generally still be claimed on top.

Bike or e-bike riders: the cents-per-km method is only for cars. Log your actual bike costs (repairs, batteries, gear) as expenses instead. Set your distance to 0 or skip the odometer.

Receipt photos (Pro): tap Receipt photo when adding an expense and take or choose a photo of the receipt. It's stored with the expense, backed up to your account, and a 📎 appears in the list — tap it to view. At tax time, Export → Download photos gives your tax agent every receipt and odometer photo in one ZIP, named to match the CSV files.

Repeats monthly (Pro): for bills like your phone plan or insurance, tick Repeats monthly and Odo adds it again on the same day each month. To stop it, delete the original entry (the one with the repeat icon and the earliest date).

💡 Keep receipts for expenses. A photo is fine. The ATO wants written evidence for most claims, and you should keep it for 5 years after you lodge.

Understanding your numbers

Tax saved by your deductions (the big odometer on Home) = your total deductions × your tax rate. It's how much less tax you'll pay because of your work claims. Delivery and rideshare apps don't take tax out of your pay, so most drivers still owe some tax at the end of the year — this lowers that bill. It is not a refund.

Put aside for tax (the card under your totals) is a rough weekly amount to save so tax time isn't a shock: your profit × your tax rate + 2% Medicare levy, spread across the weeks so far.

Distance deduction = work km × the ATO rate. For 2026–27 the rate is 91c per km (it was 88c for 2024–25 and 2025–26). Odo uses the right rate for whichever year you're viewing.

The 5,000 km cap: with the cents-per-km method you can claim at most 5,000 km per car per year (2026–27 maximum: $4,550). Odo shows when you've hit it. Full-time drivers often go well past this. If so, the logbook method may give a bigger claim, so talk to a tax agent.

Marginal tax rate (resident rates from 1 July 2026, plus 2% Medicare levy):

  • $0 – $18,200: nil
  • $18,201 – $45,000: 15%
  • $45,001 – $135,000: 30%
  • $135,001 – $190,000: 37%
  • $190,001 and over: 45%

Pick the band your total income for the year falls in, including any other job.

Income / Deductions / Net: income is everything you've logged as earnings. Net is income minus deductions. This is roughly the business profit you'll report.

GST: food delivery riders only need to register for GST once their turnover reaches $75,000 a year. Rideshare drivers (carrying passengers) must register for GST from their first dollar, whatever they earn.

Vehicles: cars, motorbikes & bikes

Set up your vehicles in Settings → Your vehicles. Give each a name and a type.

  • Car / ute / van: kilometres are claimed at the ATO cents-per-km rate, up to 5,000 km per car per year.
  • Motorbike / scooter and bicycle / e-bike: the cents-per-km method doesn't apply to these. Odo still records the distance, but you claim actual running costs (fuel, servicing, rego, insurance, repairs) as expenses at your business-use %.

Rider mode: if all your vehicles are bikes or motorbikes, Home shows quick-add buttons for the costs riders usually claim — phone and data, bike repairs and parts, e-bike batteries and charging, bags and gear, helmet and safety gear. Bicycles have no odometer, so just enter the distance from your app's trip history.

With more than one vehicle, pick which one you're using when you start or add a shift. The Home screen and your summary show the claim for each vehicle separately. Extra vehicles are part of Odo Pro.

Insights (Pro)

The Insights tab turns your shifts into decisions:

  • Earnings insights: your earnings per hour, per km and per shift, a comparison of each platform, and your best day of the week. Enter hours on your shifts to get the most out of this.
  • Best-method checker: compares the two ATO car methods. Enter the total km your car drove this year (odometer now minus your 1 July reading), plus depreciation and loan interest if you know them. Odo works out your business-use % and shows whether cents per km or the logbook method gives the bigger claim. Using the logbook method requires a 12-week logbook and receipts — talk to your tax agent before switching.
  • Tax set-aside: delivery platforms don't take tax out of your pay. Odo estimates the tax on your profit and tells you how much to put aside each week.

Free, Pro & Tax Packs

Free — always: log shifts and expenses (one live shift a day, unlimited manual entries), one vehicle, the current financial year, your tax-back estimate and cloud backup.

Pro (monthly or yearly) adds: unlimited live shifts, receipt and odometer photos, recurring expenses, multiple vehicles, every financial year and Insights (earnings, best-method checker, full tax set-aside).

Exports (CSV files, tax summary, photos ZIP) are included with yearly Pro once your free trial ends, or with monthly Pro after 3 paid months in a row.

Tax Pack — a one-off purchase that unlocks viewing and exporting for one financial year, no subscription. Handy if you're on the free or monthly plan and just need your files at tax time. Buy it from the Export tab (for the year you're viewing).

Your records always stay yours. If Pro ends, nothing is deleted: your receipts stay viewable, past years unlock again when you resubscribe, and you can download all your data anytime from Account.

💡 Pro and Tax Packs are work expenses — log them in Expenses and claim them.

Financial years

Australian financial years run from 1 July to 30 June. Odo shows one year at a time, labelled like FY 26–27.

Use the ‹ › arrows at the top of the Home screen to move between years. The Shifts, Expenses and Export tabs follow the year you choose.

💡 Lodging last year's return? Tap ‹ to go back to that year before exporting.

Tax time: exporting your records

The Export tab turns your year into files for your tax agent, or for yourself if you lodge through myTax. Exports come with yearly Pro (after the free trial) or a one-off Tax Pack for the year.

  • Shifts CSV: every shift with dates, platforms, odometer readings, km, earnings and hours. Opens in Excel or Google Sheets.
  • Expenses CSV: every expense with category, amount, business % and the deductible amount.
  • Summary: a one-page overview of income, km, deductions and estimated tax saving. Copy it into an email to your accountant.
  1. Make sure the right financial year is selected.
  2. Open Export and tap the file you need. On iPhone, choose Save to Files or share it straight to Mail.
  3. Send it to your tax agent, or use the totals when you fill in myTax (sole trader / business income section).
💡 The ATO says you need to show how you worked out your business kilometres. Your Odo shift log, with dates and odometer readings, is exactly that kind of record.

Account, backup & changing phones

Odo works without an account, but then your records live only on that phone. If you delete the app, clear Safari data or lose the phone, they're gone.

With a free account, every change is backed up automatically. The small dot on the 👤 button shows the status:

  • Green: backed up.
  • Amber: syncing right now.
  • Grey: you're offline. Odo keeps working and syncs when you're back online.
  • Red: something went wrong. Open Account and tap Backup to retry.

New phone? Install Odo, sign in with the same email, and your records come back.

Signing out removes your records from that phone (they stay safe in your account). Useful if you share a device.

Forgot your password? On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password? and follow the email link.

Your data: Account → Download my data saves everything as a file at any time, free. You can also request account deletion there.

Odo Pro subscription

See "Free vs Pro" for everything Pro includes. Logging shifts and expenses, the tax-back estimate and cloud backup stay free.

  • Free trial: try Pro free first. You won't be charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel before then.
  • Upgrade: tap Go Pro at the top of the screen, pick monthly or yearly (yearly is cheaper) and pay securely through Stripe.
  • Manage or cancel: Account → Manage subscription. You can change plan, update your card, download invoices or cancel. If you cancel, Pro keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for.
💡 Odo Pro is a work expense. Log the subscription in Expenses under Other at your business-use percentage, and claim it.

Troubleshooting & getting help

My shifts disappeared. Check the financial year at the top of Home first, since you may be viewing a different year. If you use an account, check you're signed in with the same email.

The numbers look wrong. Check Settings: region, marginal rate, and that "Use the ATO rate automatically" is ticked.

Odo looks out of date. Close it fully (swipe it away) and reopen. Odo updates itself automatically when online.

Still stuck? Go to Settings → Help & support, describe the problem and send it. If you're signed in, our reply appears on that same screen.

Leave Include diagnostics ticked. It sends technical details such as app version, phone model and recent errors, which help us fix things fast. It never includes what's in your shifts or expenses. Tap "See exactly what's sent" to check.

Important: not tax advice

Odo is a record-keeping tool. Its figures are estimates based on the rates and rules in Settings, and those change. Before you lodge, confirm current rules at ato.gov.au or with a registered tax agent. Your records, and what you claim, remain your responsibility.
