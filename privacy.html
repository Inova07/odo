1. Who we are

Odo is a shift, kilometre and expense tracker for delivery and rideshare drivers. It is operated by (ABN ), based in , Australia ("we", "us").

We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

Using Odo without an account? Your records stay on your device. We don't receive them.

3. How we use it

  • To run Odo: calculate your deductions, back up your records and sync them between your devices.
  • To manage your account and Odo Pro subscription, including trial and billing emails sent by Stripe.
  • To answer support requests and fix bugs.
  • To keep Odo secure and prevent misuse.
  • To meet our legal obligations, for example tax record-keeping for our own business.

We'll only use your information for another purpose if you agree, or if the law allows or requires it. We don't use your records for advertising or sell them to anyone.

5. Overseas storage

Our database is hosted in Australia (Sydney) where available. Some providers, including Stripe and Supabase, are based in or may process data in other countries, mainly the United States. We take reasonable steps to make sure these providers protect your information to a standard consistent with the APPs.

6. How we protect it

  • All data travels over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Database rules mean each signed-in user can access only their own records.
  • Passwords are hashed, and payment card data never touches our systems.
  • Access to the admin dashboard is limited to us and protected by strong passwords and two-factor authentication.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account and records: while your account is open. When you ask us to delete your account, we erase it within 30 days.
  • Support requests: up to 2 years, then deleted.
  • Payment and invoice records: kept by Stripe, and by us as required by Australian tax law (generally 5 years).

Tip: the ATO expects you to keep your own tax records for 5 years after you lodge. Export your records from Odo each year and keep a copy.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Access: in Odo, go to Account → Download my data to get everything we hold about your account, free, at any time.
  • Correction: edit or delete any record in the app, or ask us to correct your account details.
  • Deletion: go to Account → Delete my account, or email us. Please cancel any subscription first.
  • Diagnostics: untick "Include diagnostics" when contacting support if you prefer not to send them.
  • No account: you can use Odo's tracking features without an account, keeping your data only on your device.

We respond to requests within 30 days.

9. Cookies and device storage

Odo stores your records, settings and sign-in session in your browser's local storage so it works offline and keeps you signed in. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Stripe may set its own cookies on its checkout pages for security and fraud prevention.

10. Age requirement

Odo is designed for adults working as delivery or rideshare drivers. You must be 18 or older to create an account.

11. Data breaches

If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) as required under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Odo changes. We'll update the date at the top, and for significant changes we'll tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.

13. Contact and complaints

For privacy questions, requests or complaints, email or use Help & support in the app.

If you're not happy with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner: oaic.gov.au · 1300 363 992.
